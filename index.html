About

I teach Computer Science and Engineering at Government Polytechnic, Joida, under the Karnataka Department of Technical Education diploma curriculum. My research uses machine learning on EEG (brain-signal) data to detect and grade stress, and I am also interested in computer networks. I hold an M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (KLE Institute of Technology, 2026) and an M.Tech in Industrial Automation & Robotics (VTU, 2013).

Research interests

  • EEG signal processing and stress detection
  • Machine learning and ensemble models
  • Computer networks
