About me

I have taught Computer Science & Engineering diploma courses at Government Polytechnics in Karnataka since 2013, first at Karwar and, since 2016, at Joida. I teach Python, AI and machine learning, databases, computer networks and IoT under the Karnataka DTE curriculum.

Most of what my students learn from, I write myself: illustrated study notes, lab manuals with tested programs and their real outputs, question banks and solved model papers. Every program runs and every answer is checked before it reaches a student.

My research is about machine learning that reads people. My M.Tech dissertation, NeuroStress AI, detects stress from EEG brain signals; the lines moving across the top of this page are EEG. I have also built a driver drowsiness alarm and a face-recognition attendance system that is hard to fool with proxies.

Outside the classroom I like walking forest trails around Joida with a camera.

Projects

NeuroStress AI

Reads EEG brain signals and grades stress with an ensemble of machine learning models, shown in a Flask web app.

Python, scikit-learn, Flask. Code is private until the paper is out.

Driver drowsiness detection

Tracks 68 facial landmarks from a webcam and speaks an alert for closed eyes, yawning or nodding. Runs at 12–20 frames per second on a laptop.
